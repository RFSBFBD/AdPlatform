### Typed integrated model/problem layer

- Router checks objective/platform/campaign type/scope/data evidence/variance/shape/period/domain and routes small finite problems to exhaustive enumeration and larger finite problems to a separate typed MILP. The wider original continuous/nonlinear/QP/router policy is NOT complete.
